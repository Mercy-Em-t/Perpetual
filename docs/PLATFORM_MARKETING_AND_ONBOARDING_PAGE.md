## 1. Value Proposition Pitch (The Merchant Hook)

> ### *"Turn Your WhatsApp into a 24/7 Automated Storefront Without Losing 30% to Aggregators."*

Traditional delivery apps charge Kenyan food and retail merchants between 25% and 35% commission on every order, while withholding your customer contacts. 

With our platform:
- **You own your customer relationships**: Every customer stays in your direct WhatsApp community.
- **Zero overselling**: Order Guard automatically prevents orders when kitchen stock runs low.
- **Automated M-Pesa Tills**: No more screenshot fraud or manual Till checking. Orders clear in 3 seconds flat.
- **T+1 Morning Settlements**: Your earnings arrive in your M-Pesa or Bank Till every morning at 09:15 AM EAT.
