# Staff Operations Handbook

## Shift Hours
Floor operations run from 7:00 to 19:00, Monday to Saturday. Packers must clock in through the WhatsApp floor group with the !status command.

## Escalations
Damaged stock found during packing must be reported with !ticket so the quality desk can inspect it before dispatch.

## Leave Requests
Staff request annual leave from their department lead at least 14 days in advance. Employees receive 21 days of annual leave per year.
