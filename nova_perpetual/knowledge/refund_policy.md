# Refund Policy

## Damaged Parcels
The refund limit for a damaged parcel is USD 50 per parcel. Customers must report damage within 7 days of delivery and include a photo of the parcel and contents. Refunds above USD 50 require written approval from a store manager.

## Lost Parcels
A parcel is considered lost if it has not been delivered 10 days after dispatch. Lost parcels are refunded in full, up to the declared order value, after a trace is completed by the dispatch team.

## Late Deliveries
Late deliveries are not eligible for a cash refund. Customers receive a USD 5 store credit when a delivery arrives more than 48 hours after the promised window.

## How Refunds Are Paid
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method (M-Pesa or card) within 3 business days. Nova can issue refunds up to the USD 50 auto-approval limit; anything larger is queued for manager approval.
