# Delivery Policy

## Delivery Service
All orders are delivered by Perpetua Express, our in-house delivery service. Every parcel is tracked from the packing station to the customer's door.

## Delivery Windows
Orders inside Nairobi are delivered within 24 hours of dispatch. Orders outside Nairobi are delivered within 3 business days of dispatch.

## Order Stations
Every order moves through five stations: PENDING (awaiting payment), PAID, PACKAGING, VERIFIED (quality checked) and DISPATCHED (handed to the rider). Customers can ask Nova for the current station of any order they own.

## Delivery Fees
Delivery inside Nairobi costs USD 3. Delivery is free for orders above USD 100.
